Supreme Leader Infinite Value

How the King Moves

The King can move exactly 1 square in any direction: horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. He can never step into check (a square attacked by an enemy piece).

Special Power: Castling! The King jumps two squares toward a Rook to hide safely behind a shield of pawns.
Coach Tip: “Keep your King safe in the opening, then bring him into the center during the endgame!”
