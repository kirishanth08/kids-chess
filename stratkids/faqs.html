Detailed Answers

All Camp Questions Answered

We welcome young thinkers ages 5 through 16. To ensure age-appropriate learning, campers are grouped into four distinct developmental tracks:
  • Little Thinkers (Ages 5–7): Story-based chess, sensory games, and motor skills.
  • Smart Players (Ages 8–10): Tactical puzzles, board games, and opening basics.
  • Strategic Minds (Ages 11–13): Deep calculation, candidate moves, and tournament prep.
  • Future Masters (Ages 14–16): Master repertoires, time control analysis, and leadership.

Absolutely not! Over 60% of our incoming campers have never touched a chess piece before Day 1. Our coaches are specialists in welcoming true beginners with gentle, encouraging stories that make piece movements instinctive and fun.

Campers should bring:
  • A labeled backpack
  • A refillable water bottle (filtered stations available throughout campus)
  • Comfortable sneakers for outdoor lawn recess
  • A packed lunch (if enrolled in full-day; peanut & tree nut-free)
  • All chess equipment, tactics workbooks, and t-shirts are provided by StratKids on Day 1!

Yes, we provide two organic fruit snacks and chilled electrolyte beverages daily (mid-morning and mid-afternoon). For lunch, full-day campers bring their own packed lunch to accommodate specific family dietary preferences. All dining areas maintain strict 100% nut-free allergen separation.

On Monday morning, coaches conduct gentle, non-stressful mini-puzzle assessments disguised as warm-up games. Campers are then grouped into small pods of 6 children with peers of matching tactical comfort so no one feels overwhelmed or held back.

Our Friday Grand Prix is strictly non-elimination and celebratory! Every child plays four rounds regardless of outcomes, earns points toward their team pod, and receives an engraved metal medal and certificate during our family awards ceremony.

Yes! Siblings share common morning drop-off, lunchtime outdoor recess, and afternoon pickup, while attending classroom instruction tailored to their respective age tracks. Plus, enjoy our automatic 15% Sibling Discount!
