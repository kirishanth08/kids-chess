Daily Camp Routine

A Day at StratKids

Structured, energetic, and engaging. Designed to balance focused strategic learning with outdoor play, socialization, and rewarding puzzle challenges.

09:00 – 09:30 AM

Welcome & Mind Warm-Up

Campers arrive, check in with mentors, and start the day with light mini-chess puzzles, riddles, and group warm-up games.

09:30 – 11:00 AM

Chess Fundamentals & Master Lesson

Coach-led master demonstration board lesson. Campers practice forks, pins, skewers, and opening defense in small skill pods.

11:00 – 12:00 PM

Strategy Board Game Challenge

Transition to tactical tabletop games such as Blokus, Hive, and Quoridor to apply spatial thinking in different rule sets.

12:00 – 01:30 PM

Healthy Lunch & Outdoor Play

Nutritious lunch break followed by supervised outdoor games, lawn sports, and casual social bonding in the park sunshine.

01:30 – 03:00 PM

Friendly Camp Tournament Practice

Campers play rated practice games using digital clocks. Coaches provide constructive live analysis and encouraging feedback.

03:00 – 04:30 PM

Puzzle Relay, Wrap-Up & Daily Badges

High-energy team puzzle race, daily badge ceremony celebrating sportsmanship and creative moves, followed by parent pickup.
