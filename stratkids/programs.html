5-Level Skill Architecture

The StratKids Skill Progression System

Campers progress at their own pace through structured milestones without feeling rushed or judged.

Beginner
Coordinates & Values

Board files & ranks, piece movement paths, checkmate vs stalemate, and King protection.

Foundation
Center Control & Forks

Opening rules, minor piece development, castling defense, and spotting elementary 1-move forks.

Intermediate
Tactical Combinations

Pins, skewers, discovered attacks, pawn breaks, candidate move trees, and clock play.

Advanced
Positional Strategy

Outpost squares, King & Pawn endgames, prophylaxis, and tournament Swiss pairing rules.

Young Strategist
Mastery & Leadership

Full opening repertoires, tournament resilience, sportsmanship honors, and peer mentorship.
