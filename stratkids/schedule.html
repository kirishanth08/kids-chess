Smooth & Safe Operations

Drop-Off & Dismissal Procedures

Safe, swift, and stress-free for working parents.

Curbside Drop-Off (08:45–09:00 AM)

Our camp counselors greet campers directly at the car drop-off lane. Parents do not need to hunt for parking.

Digital PIN Security Dismissal

Authorized pickups enter their unique 4-digit parent security PIN generated in the StratKids Parent Portal app.

Flexible Late Stay Options

Stuck in traffic? Extended care is available until 06:00 PM with supervised board games and healthy snacks.
