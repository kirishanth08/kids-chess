The Origin

Born From a Passion for Young Minds

StratKids was founded by a coalition of national chess masters and primary school educators who realized traditional chess clubs were often too rigid or intimidating for young children.

We reimagined chess into an adventurous summer camp experience where friendly piece characters, colorful puzzle boards, tabletop strategy games, and sunny outdoor recess combine into an unforgettable summer adventure.

Our North Star

Every child leaves StratKids believing in their ability to solve any challenge — on and off the board — with patience, confidence, and resilience.

Core Pedagogy

The 4 Pillars of StratKids Mentorship

We treat strategy as an empowering life habit rather than just winning pieces.

Pause & Visualize

We teach campers to take their hands off the piece, pause for two deep breaths, and visualize opponent responses before committing.

Mistakes Are Data

Losing a Queen is not a failure; it is an exciting clue. Campers analyze lost games with curiosity and zero shame.

Honor Opponents

Every match begins and ends with an encouraging handshake: “Good luck, have fun” and “Thank you for helping me learn.”

Growth Over Trophies

We celebrate creative ideas, sportsmanship badges, and daily perseverance just as passionately as tournament checkmates.

Accreditation & Care

The StratKids Safety Guarantee

Every campus facility follows stringent national safety standards to ensure parents have absolute peace of mind throughout the day.

1:6 Camper-to-Coach Ratio

Strict class caps guarantee that no child is overlooked. Small pods allow personalized coaching and attentive supervision during every activity.

Secure Gate & PIN Dismissal

Single-entry campus doors with photo ID checks and parent mobile PIN verification for morning drop-off and afternoon pickup.

CPR / Pediatric First Aid

Full-time certified medical personnel on-site, 100% nut-free allergen dining tables, and strict hydration monitoring during outdoor recess.
