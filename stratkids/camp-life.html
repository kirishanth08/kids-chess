The Daily Experience

Where Every Day Has a New Challenge.

Camp at StratKids is far more than sitting quietly at a chessboard. It's an electric mix of collaborative tactical puzzles, modern tabletop strategy games, team relays, and sunshine lawn sports!

Interactive Tactics Lab

Morning Brain Spark

Every morning begins with high-definition master demonstration boards. Campers gather around as coaches show how grandmasters calculate 3 moves ahead, dissecting openings and spotting decoy sacrifices.

10 Daily Puzzles Annotated In Book

Cognitive Mastery

Hands-On Tactics & Clock Strategy

Campers don't merely watch lessons; they immediately apply motifs in paired practice games. Coaches wander between boards offering real-time hints: “Look at Black's back rank—is their King protected?”

This rapid feedback loop turns abstract positional theory into instinctive pattern recognition that sticks with children for life.

Beyond the 64 Squares

The Tabletop Strategy Arena

We integrate award-winning modern board games to broaden strategic cognition into spatial geometry, resource budgeting, and diplomatic cooperation.

Blokus & Hive
Mastering polyomino territorial blocks and organic surround tactics without a fixed board.
Quoridor Mazes
Navigating labyrinth paths while deciding between advancing your pawn or blocking your rival.
Catan Junior
Resource allocation, trade negotiations, and building supply chains across pirate islands.
Set & Dobble
Lightning visual processing: detecting color, shading, and number patterns in milliseconds.
Spirited Team Energy

Puzzle Relays: Strategy at Full Speed

Who said chess has to be stationary? Every afternoon, campers split into color squads for the legendary StratKids Puzzle Relay. Pairs sprint up to magnetic boards, deliberate for 15 seconds, execute a tactical move, and tag their teammates!

Fosters True Team Camaraderie Campers cheer each other on, learning that collective brainpower wins the relay.
Fresh Air & Sunshine

Outdoor Lawn Games & Mind Breaks

Mental stamina requires healthy physical movement. Every day includes a dedicated 60-to-75 minute outdoor recess on secured green fields.

Capture-the-King

Our chess-themed version of capture the flag! Campers designate Knights and Castles to tag opponents and retrieve the royal scepter.

Frisbee Tactical Runs

Passing discs across designated zones while anticipating interceptors, translating chess diagonals into open field sports.

Hydration & Sun Safety

Full counselor supervision, mandatory water breaks every 20 minutes, shaded pavilion resting zones, and SPF sunscreen reapplication.

Creative Expression

Creative Board Art & Custom Pieces

Every Thursday, campers enjoy our Strategy Craft Lab. They paint custom wooden Pawns, design their own personalized fantasy chessboard squares, and collect colorful silicone achievement wristbands.
