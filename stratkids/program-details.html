Program Explanation

Understanding The Little Thinkers Experience

The Little Thinkers Discovery Pod is crafted specifically for kindergarten and early elementary children who are taking their very first steps onto the 64 squares. We translate complex rules into joyful physical movement, giant felt board games, and character-driven tales.

Instead of memorizing notation, campers help the Hopping Knight rescue friends and watch Castle Rooks roll down open highways. Every 30-minute lesson is balanced with sensory tabletop logic games, tactile chess crafts, and energetic outdoor recess.

Key Highlights of This Track
  • Character-driven rules: Knight leaps, Rook walls, Bishop diagonals
  • Giant 3-foot tactile floor boards for physical spatial learning
  • Dedicated 1:6 coach ratio with early-childhood certified mentors
  • Daily milestone sticker ceremonies and non-elimination play
